TENS

house rules · shedding game
House rules recap: match or go lower, never higher. Hand and face-up cards playable any time; face-down unlocks the instant your 4 face-up cards are gone. Tens and four-of-a-kind burn the pile. Can't play? Draw from the deck instead of a forced pickup — and if you do have to pick up, you go again. 10s cost 50 points if left in hand at game end, face cards cost 10, others face value.
